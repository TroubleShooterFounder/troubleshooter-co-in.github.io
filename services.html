e-Discovery Services

Electronic Discovery for Modern Litigation

Our e-Discovery services cover the entire EDRM (Electronic Discovery Reference Model) lifecycle. We help organizations and law firms efficiently manage electronically stored information (ESI) for litigation, regulatory compliance, and investigations.

What We Offer:

Data Identification & Collection

Forensically sound collection of data from various sources including emails, databases, cloud storage, and mobile devices.

Processing & Analytics

Advanced processing using de-duplication, near-duplicate detection, email threading, and predictive coding.

Review & Analysis

AI-assisted review combined with expert human reviewers for accuracy and efficiency.

Production

Production in all standard formats including TIFF, PDF, Native, with full Bates numbering and redaction support.
