Advanced electronic discovery solutions for litigation, regulatory compliance, and internal investigations. We manage the complete EDRM lifecycle to deliver accurate, defensible, and cost-effective results.

Service Overview

Complete EDRM Lifecycle Management

From identification to production, we handle every stage of the e-Discovery process

Data Identification & Collection

Forensically sound collection of data from emails, databases, cloud storage, mobile devices, and other sources while maintaining chain of custody.

Processing & Analytics

Advanced processing using de-duplication, near-duplicate detection, email threading, and predictive coding to reduce review volume.

Review & Analysis

AI-assisted review combined with expert human reviewers for maximum accuracy, efficiency, and defensibility in court.

Production & Delivery

Production in all standard formats including TIFF, PDF, Native, with Bates numbering, redaction, and privilege log support.

Legal Hold Management

Automated legal hold notifications, tracking, and compliance management to ensure preservation of relevant data.

Project Management

Dedicated project managers ensure timely delivery, transparent communication, and budget control throughout the process.
