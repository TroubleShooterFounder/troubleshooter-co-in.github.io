Your Privacy Matters to Us

This Privacy Policy explains how Trouble Shooters ("we", "our", "us"), a sole proprietorship providing Cyber Security, e-Discovery, Digital Forensics, and IT Support services in India, collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you visit our website at troubleshooter.co.in or engage with our services. Please read this policy carefully before using our services.

1

Introduction

Trouble Shooters is committed to safeguarding the privacy of all individuals who interact with our website, services, and communications. This policy applies to all visitors, clients, prospective clients, job applicants, and anyone who contacts us through any channel.

As a cyber security firm, we understand the critical importance of data privacy better than anyone. We practice what we preach — the same security standards we recommend to our clients are applied rigorously to how we handle your data.

Note: By accessing our website or using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our website and services.

2

Information We Collect

We collect only the information that is necessary to provide our services and improve your experience. This falls into two categories:

👤

Information You Provide

Data you voluntarily submit through forms, emails, calls, or other direct interactions with us.

🤖

Automatically Collected

Technical data collected automatically when you browse our website, such as IP address and browser type.

Information you provide directly:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number (via contact or inquiry forms)
  • Company name, designation, and business details (for corporate clients)
  • Details of your cyber security issue or service requirement
  • Resume, cover letter, and professional history (for job applicants)
  • Communication history — emails, WhatsApp messages, and call records
  • Payment and billing information (processed through secure third-party gateways)
  • Any attachments or documents shared with us for service purposes

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, and navigation patterns on our website
  • Referral URLs (the page that brought you to our site)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet) and screen resolution
  • Cookie data and session identifiers (see Section 10)

We do NOT collect: Sensitive personal data such as Aadhaar numbers, PAN, financial account credentials, biometric data, or health information unless specifically required for a contracted forensic or legal service and with your explicit consent.

3

How We Use Your Data

Your personal information is used solely for legitimate business purposes. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties for marketing.

Primary purposes:

  • Responding to your inquiries, quote requests, and service consultations
  • Providing and managing cyber security, e-Discovery, and IT support services
  • Processing and tracking job applications for open positions
  • Sending service-related communications, updates, and reports
  • Generating invoices and processing payments for completed services
  • Fulfilling legal and contractual obligations under service agreements

Secondary purposes:

  • Improving our website experience, content, and navigation
  • Analyzing website traffic and user behavior through analytics tools
  • Sending newsletters, blog updates, or security advisories (only with consent)
  • Preventing fraud, unauthorized access, and ensuring website security
  • Complying with applicable laws, court orders, and regulatory requirements

Legal Basis: We process your data based on (a) your consent, (b) contractual necessity, (c) legitimate business interests, or (d) compliance with legal obligations — as applicable under the DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR.

4

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We treat your data with strict confidentiality. Your personal information is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

🔧

Service Providers

Trusted third-party vendors who assist in delivering our services (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processing, email delivery) under strict data processing agreements.

⚖️

Legal Requirements

When required by law, court order, government authority, or to protect our legal rights — we may disclose data as legally obligated.

🤝

With Your Consent

Any sharing beyond the above will only happen with your explicit written consent. We will always inform you before sharing your data.

🏢

Business Transfers

In the unlikely event of a merger or acquisition, your data may transfer to the successor entity, subject to the same privacy protections.

We never sell your data. Trouble Shooters does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing, advertising, or commercial purposes — ever.

5

Data Security

As a cyber security firm, data protection is at the core of everything we do. We implement industry-leading security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • All data transmitted via our website is protected with TLS/SSL encryption
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments of our systems
  • Secure data storage with encrypted backups and access logging
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all internal systems
  • Staff trained in data privacy best practices and confidentiality obligations
  • Incident response protocols for potential data breach scenarios

Important: While we employ robust security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you have reason to believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately at info@troubleshooter.co.in.

6

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

  • Client service data: Retained for the duration of the engagement + 5 years (for legal compliance)
  • Contact form inquiries: Retained for 2 years from the date of last communication
  • Job applications: Retained for 12 months after the position is filled; you may request deletion anytime
  • Website analytics data: Aggregated and anonymized data retained for up to 26 months
  • Financial records: Retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax and accounting laws
  • Marketing consent records: Retained until you withdraw consent

After the retention period expires, your data is securely deleted or anonymized. You may request early deletion of your data subject to legal retention requirements (see Section 7).

8

DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India): Trouble Shooters is committed to full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — the landmark legislation governing how organizations collect and process personal data of Indian citizens.

In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023, Trouble Shooters:

  • Collects personal data only for specified, clear, and lawful purposes with your consent
  • Processes data only to the extent necessary for the stated purpose (data minimization)
  • Maintains reasonable security safeguards to prevent data breaches
  • Notifies affected individuals and the Data Protection Board of India in the event of a breach
  • Honors all data principal rights including access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal
  • Appoints a Consent Manager and maintains records of all consents received
  • Does not process personal data of children under 18 without verifiable parental consent
  • Ensures cross-border data transfers comply with government-notified whitelisted countries

For any DPDP Act-related queries, grievances, or consent withdrawal, contact us at privacy@troubleshooter.co.in . We will acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days.

9

GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

For EU/EEA Residents: If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, your data is handled in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — Regulation 2016/679).

For EU/EEA data subjects, the following additional protections apply:

  • Lawful basis for processing is documented for each data processing activity
  • Data transfers outside the EU are conducted under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or other approved mechanisms
  • You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (DPA)
  • We appoint an EU Representative for GDPR compliance purposes where required
  • Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) are conducted for high-risk processing activities
  • We maintain a Record of Processing Activities (RoPA) as required by Article 30

While our primary operations are in India and governed by the DPDP Act 2023, we respect GDPR principles as a global data privacy standard and apply them wherever applicable.

Cookie Policy

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit our website.

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
_session_id Essential Maintains your session state while browsing Session
_ga, _gid Analytics Google Analytics — tracks page views and traffic (anonymized) 2 years / 24 hrs
_gat Analytics Google Analytics — throttles request rate 1 minute
cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preference 1 year
_fbp Marketing Facebook Pixel — tracks conversions (only if enabled) 3 months

Managing Cookies: You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies from being set
  • Block all cookies from being set (this may break some website features)
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Analytics Opt-Out: You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.
